[Overview **Ret2PLT** is a **technique** where instead of jumping directly to a libc function (which requires knowing libc's base address), you jump to its PLT stub inside the binary. Since PLT addresses are fixed in non-**PIE** binaries, this works regardless of ASLR. **Ret2PLT** is most commonly used to leak libc addresses as part of a two-stage **exploit**.](https://duckduckgo.com/l/?uddg=https%3A%2F%2Fgithub.com%2Falhamrizvi%2Dcloud%2FBinary%2DExploitation%2Fblob%2Fmain%2F08.%2520Ret2Plt%2FREADME.md&rut=df071b68bf9be5d7925937f50545cc94abca717c20a0492a525f0027516ca75d)
