# Superseded negative rerun

This directory is the earlier public rerun with the stock early A1
(`a1_early.bin`). It reached only a parse-time OOM (`tried to allocate 8192
bytes`) and B1 returned no pointers (`decoded=34`). It produced no
target-written proof.

It is preserved as historical evidence and is **superseded** by the
recovered-body rerun recorded in `../report.md` and `../proof/`. Do not read
this directory as the proof for 8.2.34.
